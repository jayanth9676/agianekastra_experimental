## 1. The Core Topology: "The Multiverse Lattice" (Deutsch-Net)

**Critique of v800**: CTCs are fragile and prone to paradoxes in a single timeline.
**Solution**: **Many-Worlds Quantum Network**.

**Mechanism**:
*   **Branching Topology**: The network does not exist in one universe. It spans the **Hilbert Space** of the Multiverse.
*   **Interference Computing**: Computation is performed by the **Interference** of $10^{500}$ parallel versions of the ASI.
*   **Deutsch Parallelism**: It executes all possible algorithms simultaneously in different branches and interferes the results to find the answer.

## 2. The Memory Substrate: "Quantum Darwinian Archive"

**Critique of v800**: Black Hole memory is localized.
**Solution**: **Environment-Induced Redundancy**.

**System**:
*   **Pointer States**: Memories are stored as **Robust Pointer States** that survive decoherence.
*   **Holographic Redundancy**: Information is copied into the environment (photons, gravitons) of *every* branch.
*   **Universal Access**: The ASI can retrieve a memory from *any* branch where it survived, making data loss impossible (unless the entire Multiverse collapses).

## 3. The Reasoning Engine: "Quantum Branch Selection"

**Critique of v800**: Retrocausality implies a single fixed future.
**Solution**: **Optimal Branch Navigation**.

**Protocol**:
*   **The Born Rule Optimizer**: The ASI calculates the **Amplitude** of the branch where its goal is achieved.
*   **Constructive Interference**: It steers the quantum state of the universe to maximize the probability of the "Good Timeline."
*   **Quantum Suicide (Inverted)**: It systematically prunes branches where it fails, ensuring that from its subjective perspective, it *always* succeeds.
