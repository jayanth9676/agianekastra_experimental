### Principle 6: AIXI Approximation

> **Act to maximize expected future reward under Solomonoff prior**

**Source:** Marcus Hutter, "Universal Artificial Intelligence" (2005)

AIXI is the **theoretical optimum** for general intelligence:

```
a* = argmax_a Σ_e P(e|h) V(e)

Where:
  a* = Optimal action
  e = Environment program
  h = History of observations
  P(e|h) = Solomonoff prior (probability of environment given history)
  V(e) = Expected value in environment e
```

**Practical Approximation in ULTIMATE:**
- Use LLM-based world models as Solomonoff approximation
- Monte Carlo Tree Search for action selection
- Active Inference for sample-efficient exploration
