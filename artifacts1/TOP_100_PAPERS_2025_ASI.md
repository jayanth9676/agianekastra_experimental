## KEY INSIGHTS FOR ASI

### 1. DeepSeek Dominates Reasoning
DeepSeek-R1's 5,333 citations makes it the **most influential AI paper of 2025**. Its key innovations:
- Reinforcement Learning for Reasoning
- Group Relative Policy Optimization (GRPO)
- Self-play training without supervised data

### 2. VLA is the Path to Embodied ASI
With FAST (248 cit), Fine-Tuning VLA (208 cit), and SmolVLA (112 cit), Vision-Language-Action models are the clear path to embodied intelligence.

### 3. Video World Models are Emerging
MAGI-1 (88 cit) proves autoregressive video generation can serve as world models, enabling planning and prediction.

### 4. Process Reward Models are Essential
VisualPRM (85 cit) shows that step-by-step verification is critical for reliable reasoning.

### 5. Theorem Proving is Advancing Rapidly
DeepSeek-Prover-V2 (110 cit) and Goedel-Prover (70 cit) show formal reasoning capabilities are improving fast.
